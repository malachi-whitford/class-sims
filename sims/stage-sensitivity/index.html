Which Life Stage Matters Most?

Loggerhead sea turtles were declining. Conservation had focused on protecting eggs on nesting beaches. A stage-based population model asks: if you could improve survival in just one life stage, which one would do the most good? Tap stages to protect them.

Stage survival rates, stage lengths, and egg numbers are from the loggerhead model of Crouse, Crowder, and Caswell for turtles nesting on Little Cumberland Island, Georgia. The result helped make turtle excluder devices (TEDs) on shrimp trawls required in U.S. waters.
Crouse, D. T., L. B. Crowder, & H. Caswell (1987). A stage-based population model for loggerhead sea turtles and implications for conservation. Ecology 68: 1412–1423.
