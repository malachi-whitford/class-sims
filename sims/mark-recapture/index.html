Mark–Recapture

How many fish are in a pond you can't see into? Catch some, mark them, let them go. Later, catch another sample: the share of marked fish in it tells you how big the whole population is.

Why it works: if 1 in 5 of your second catch is marked, then the M fish you marked are about 1 in 5 of the whole pond, so N ≈ 5 × M. The Chapman version, (M+1)(C+1)/(R+1) − 1, is less biased with small samples.
