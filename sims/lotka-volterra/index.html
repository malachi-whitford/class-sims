Lotka–Volterra Models

Two simple pairs of equations that describe how two species affect each other: one eats the other, or both compete for the same food.

dH/dt = bH − aHL     dL/dt = c·aHL − mL

H = snowshoe hares, L = lynx. Hares are born at rate b and eaten at rate a per lynx; each hare eaten adds c lynx; lynx die at rate m.
