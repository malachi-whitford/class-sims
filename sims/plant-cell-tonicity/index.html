Osmolarity and Tonicity: Plant Cells

Each cell has a fixed amount of solute in its vacuole (600 mOsm when flaccid). Water moves by osmosis according to water potential (Ψ = Ψs + Ψp). Change the outside solution and watch the cell respond over a few seconds.
