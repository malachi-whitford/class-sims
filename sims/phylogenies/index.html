Reading Phylogenies: California Frogs, Kangaroo Rats, and Squirrels

A phylogeny (evolutionary tree) shows how species are related by common ancestry. Species at the tips are living today; each branching point (node) is a common ancestor. Relatedness comes only from the branching pattern: two species are more closely related when they share a more recent common ancestor, no matter how close their names sit on the page.
