Soil pH: Hydrogen Adsorption and Desorption

Soil particles carry negative charge that holds cations (cation exchange). When pH falls, H⁺ and Al³⁺ are adsorbed and push base cations off the surface. When pH rises, H⁺ desorbs and base cations take its place. Compare two soils side by side.
