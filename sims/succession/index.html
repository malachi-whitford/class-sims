Fire suppression and megafires

A stand of Sierra Nevada mixed-conifer forest. Before 1900, lightning and Native American burning set low-intensity fires every 5 to 20 years. Since about 1910, nearly every fire has been put out. Watch what builds up, then see what a fire in a drought year does.
