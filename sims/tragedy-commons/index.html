Tragedy of the Commons

Five herders share one pasture. Every extra cow earns its owner money, but the grass it eats is lost to everyone. You are the blue herder; the other four each add a cow whenever it pays them. What happens to the pasture?

Garrett Hardin's 1968 essay made the shared pasture a famous metaphor for overfishing, groundwater pumping, and air pollution. Elinor Ostrom later showed that many real communities avoid the tragedy with their own rules, and won the 2009 Nobel Prize in economics for it.
Hardin, G. (1968). The tragedy of the commons. Science 162: 1243–1248.
