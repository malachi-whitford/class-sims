Who makes the trash? Residential vs. commercial vs. self-haul

CalRecycle doesn't split each county's trash by who threw it away, but its 2021 statewide study sampled garbage trucks at landfills to estimate it. Industrial businesses are counted inside "commercial." Switch to the Fresno County estimate to apply the statewide shares to Fresno County's 2025 trash total.

What's in the trash? (California, 2021)

Think about it
  1. Commercial and multifamily sources produce the largest share. Why might businesses and apartments throw away more than single-family homes?
  2. Organics are the biggest material class in the trash. Since 2022, California law (SB 1383) has required cities to collect organics separately. Why would the state target organics first?
