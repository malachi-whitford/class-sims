Rank-abundance curves

Each site's species are ranked from most to least common. A long, flat line means many species with similar abundances (rich and even). A short, steep line means few species with one or two dominating.

Only species present at this site (n > 0) are listed, because absent species add nothing to these sums.

Alpha, beta, and gamma diversity (species richness)

Same idea with Shannon diversity (effective number of species)

Richness ignores abundance. Converting Shannon's H′ to an effective number of species (e^H′, Jost 2007) lets abundance count too, and the parts still multiply: γ = α × β. Sites are weighted equally.
